# The worlds

Sonder is a universe simulator — and it does not matter whether the
universe is a spacefaring civilization or an office building. We are
trying to simulate a universe, whatever that universe may be. The
project's destination is and remains the universal space sim; the
other worlds here are evals — deliberately shallow universes that
keep the engine honest and enable the destination. This directory is
where the universes live, one home per world, each holding its
charter and, as it grows, its own shelf of lore-as-evals.

The standing law (card 160, the three-universes litmus): anything we
build must serve every world here. If it cannot, it is
world-specific content; if it can — like the random number
generator — it is a framework-level addition. And the eval practice
runs in every universe alike: an invented civilization, kingdom, or
employee is never a dictation that it exist. It is an example of
what the system must be able to support. A mechanic that cannot host
a chartered story has failed a test.

Current worlds:

- **the space world** — the Vessari and the Khedrun; the first
  world, the seed of the project's destination, and the regression
  anchor for the engine itself (its golden seal must not move while
  the framework generalizes). Its shelf
  currently lives at `docs/lore/` — the original home, from before
  the shelf went plural; whether it moves here is a docs-sweep
  decision, not a silent one.
- **[the continent](continent/charter.md)** — four to six fresh
  civilizations on one landmass, bordering one another. The spatial
  stress: contiguity, chokepoints, bilateral trade, no exchange.
- **[the office](office/charter.md)** — one employer, twenty-odd
  individual minds, building a business together. The conceptual
  stress: social distance, an open economy, and the notable-figures
  zoom tier arriving early.
