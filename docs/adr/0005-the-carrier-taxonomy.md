# ADR 0005 — The carrier taxonomy and the witness rule

Status: accepted (card 161). Extends ADR 0004. Retires the field
model from doctrine; its implementation retirement is cards
150–159, per the build map in notebook 161.

## Context

Card 122 delivered news at ship speed through a licensed shortcut:
the field — news radiating at one uniform speed with no emitter, no
vehicle, and no failure. Mike's doctrine, on the record the day it
was licensed: *nothing arrives that nothing carried.* Card 161 is
the research card that designed the field's successors on paper,
against all three worlds at once, per ADR 0004's litmus — a carrier
is a hull, a caravan, and an email before its card is even cut. The
standing acceptance test, Mike's own: payment as credits in a hull
(seven days) versus payment wired electronically (seconds) must be
the same two events at different gaps.

## Decision

**Movement is a system, and the system owns the properties.**
Everything that crosses distance — matter and information alike —
rides a *mechanism*: a row in one taxonomy. The distinction between
goods and news is a property of the cargo, not a second taxonomy:
**net-zero** payloads are conserved (grain leaving the granary is
gone from the granary; the audit's road ledger watches), while
**copyable** payloads reproduce (telling costs the teller nothing;
belief semantics watch).

Every mechanism's row has five columns:

- **speed** — distance crossed per tick; the divisor in the gap
  derivation (card 122's split stands: distance is the map's,
  speed the mover's, time-to-deliver derived and never stored);
- **coverage** — which destinations, named how, *available when*
  (schedules, at-will, one-shot);
- **failure profile** — what can go wrong in flight, including a
  declared **threat surface**: what "attack" even means on this
  mechanism (pirates board hulls; hackers hack wires; neither can
  work the other's lane);
- **cost** — who pays, in what, when (deliberately qualitative
  until a mechanic needs numbers);
- **owner** — nobody (physics), an institution, or the sender's own
  people; the owner is where bias, refusal, and selection attach.

Three structural rules bind the rows:

1. **Rows are state, not constants.** Every cell is mutable by
   events (a pass closes, a lattice is cut, a channel is jammed),
   and the row set itself is history: mechanisms are invented,
   improve, and die. News of a new mechanism travels on the old
   ones.
2. **Mechanisms are data, never code.** One carriage code path in
   the engine; a mechanism is a configuration of it. Row schema is
   engine; row instances are world content (ADR 0004's split). A
   mechanism that seems to need its own code path is a missing
   general mechanic — and becomes a card.
3. **No universal fallback.** The union of coverages need not cover
   the world; unreachability stays expressible (the shellsea), or
   ignorance stops being free. A quiet everywhere-mechanism would
   un-retire the field.

**Two delivery shapes, not three.** **Addressed** (to a name the
map can price — and an address can sail) and **radiated** (into a
neighborhood of the map, whatever the map means by nearness).
Relay is *behavior*, not a shape: a retelling is a fresh emission,
cause-linked, re-composed from the teller's beliefs and agenda. A
rumor is not one shipment; it is one *plus* shipments.

**The witness rule.** An event's news exists only in the minds that
caught it. Natural media (the world's signal medium, range scaled
by loudness) are how witnesses witness — physics offers, minds
catch — and everything past the catch is somebody carrying. No
witness, no news, forever. Law 2 is untouched: the annals records
every tree that falls; it is the *propagating copy* that needs a
carrier. Factions choose mechanisms from the menu they *believe*
exists (law 3): fastest, cheapest, safest *that they are aware of*.

**Carriage obligations.** Hiring a carrier is the ordinary trade
grammar; payloads sit in the carrier's **custody** while carried
(the road ledger's on-road column names whose hands); the carrier
always has **manifest knowledge** (what it hauls) but content
knowledge of copyable cargo is behind a *behavior* — whether that
civilization opens the mail is temperament, in the threat surface.

## Migration

Three rungs, climbed per world — derived from world independence,
not decreed: (0) this card re-describes what runs as degenerate
rows (the courier's deliver-everything-everywhere is the **field
row**: a natural medium with infinite range; journeys are addressed
self-owned mechanisms) — zero code, three seals untouched; (1) card
150 lands the row schema and re-expresses current behavior as data,
proven by bit-identical seals (the card-153 adoption pattern);
(2) each world deliberately retires its field row for real
mechanisms — one seal re-cut per world, loudly, with a ledger
entry. The witness rule is enforceable in a world only at its
rung 2; until then the field row remains the licensed placeholder.

## Consequences

Early warning becomes a differential phenomenon with three legs —
speed, price, and knowledge of channels (card 150's requirement:
warnings can outrun swords). Espionage gets physics: being the only
witness is an asset. Unwitnessed history becomes possible — the
universe keeps truths no civilization will ever hold, and silent
loss (the vanished armada) is first-class. Theft by carrier is
coherent and auditable, and produces whodunnits built from
inference instead of news. The Marrow Fleet is hostable as written:
routes, prices, biases, and a carrier who is somebody. And the
successor cards inherit assigned seams instead of a pile: wear to
151, agendas to 152, off-exchange trade to 154, plural exchanges to
155, forgery to 156, reception to 157, in-flight owners to 158,
ledger-money to 159, insurance parked at 162.
